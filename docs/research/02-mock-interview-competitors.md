# Mock Interview Competitors - Research (as of 2026-10-20)

Confidence note: official pricing pages were mostly NOT directly verified. Many sources are competitor/affiliate blogs (OphyAI, FinalRound blog, PracHub, AceRound, Verve blog). Prices conflict across sources; re-verify before any pricing decision. Reddit threads were not retrievable via search; complaints below come from Trustpilot/G2/review blogs.

## 1. Competitor profiles

### Google Interview Warmup (free)
- Modes: voice or typed; real-time transcription. Audio/transcripts not saved (can copy/download).
- Questions: pick a field (IT support, data analytics, etc.); 5 industry-specific questions (background, situational, technical). Not tailored to a JD/resume.
- Feedback: NO score/grade. Shows repeated words, job-related terms used, talking points covered; you re-answer to improve. No model answers.
- Sources: https://grow.google/interview-warmup , https://www.vineresources.com/post/practice-makes-perfect-google-039-s-interview-warmup

### Final Round AI
- Modes: live copilot + mock interviews (voice/video), resume tools. Both practice and "stealth" live assistance.
- Pricing (conflicting): ~$150/mo, ~$25/mo billed $300/yr, quarterly ~$250, MAX $500/yr; no free trial; 72h money-back claim.
- Complaints (Trustpilot ~249 reviews, Feb-Mar 2026): bugs, unresponsive support, refused refunds, rebilling; some feedback called ambiguous. A G2 reviewer praises flagging rambling/weak structure.
- Ethics: markets live copilot -> NOT a model for us.
- Sources: https://fr-be.trustpilot.com/review/finalroundai.com , https://ophyai.com/blog/career-advice/final-round-ai-pricing , https://www.g2.com/survey_responses/final-round-ai-review-12699738

### Yoodli
- Modes: voice/video speech coach + AI roleplay avatars (strong in sales/enterprise).
- Feedback: filler words, pacing, eye contact, vocabulary diversity, talk-to-listen ratio, conciseness; custom rubrics for teams. Counted metrics, not guessed.
- Pricing (unverified): free / ~$8 Pro / ~$20 Advanced individual; enterprise per-seat. G2 ~4.7 (8 reviews). Complaints: stiff personas, thin scenario library; 72h trial cap.
- Sources: https://www.outdoo.ai/blog/yoodli-review-and-pricing , https://www.capterra.com/p/10019324/Yoodli/reviews/

### Big Interview
- Modes: video practice simulator, answer builder, lessons; AI feedback on delivery, body language, content. Education/career-center focus.
- Pricing (unverified): team ~$9.75/user/mo; consumer ~$39/mo or $299 lifetime in one roundup. Aggregator rating 2.2/5 (129 reviews, low confidence). Cannot validate technical answers.
- Sources: https://www.capterra.com/p/10039230/Big-Interview/ , https://prachub.com/resources/big-interview-review-2026-is-ai-video-practice-useful-for-technical-candidates

### Huru
- Modes: voice/video, mobile-strong. Paste JD or job URL -> tailored mock interview; delivery analysis; feedback report.
- Pricing: ~$24.99/mo or ~$99/yr; trial reportedly a 5-question session; non-refundable; no offline mode. No live copilot.
- Sources: https://www.itechguides.com/products/huru/ , https://claveprep.com/blog/claveprep-vs-huru (competitor)

### Interview Sidekick
- Modes: voice mock interviews with adaptive follow-ups, 10,000+ question bank, transcripts; ALSO live copilot with "Stealth Mode".
- Pricing varies by source ($40-$60/mo; site showed $80 promo / $159 per 90 days; free 2-min test sessions). Complaint: generic suggestions on complex questions.
- Sources: https://prachub.com/resources/interview-sidekick-review-2026-live-copilot-or-real-interview-practice , https://ophyai.com/blog/career-advice/interview-sidekick-review

### Verve AI / LockedIn AI (live interview copilots)
- Primarily real-time answer overlays hidden from screen share. Verve ~$14-$35/mo (conflicting; a competitor claims ~$59.50 Pro). LockedIn ~$30-$70/mo; a competitor alleges a help article titled "How to share the screen without being caught" and non-refundable plans.
- Ethics: core issue is undisclosed outside assistance in live interviews; may breach employer rules or cause rescinded offers. Our product must be explicitly practice-only.
- Sources: https://prachub.com/resources/verve-ai-review-2026-practice-tool-or-risky-live-interview-copilot , https://www.aceround.app/blog/aceround-vs-lockedin-ai/

### Interviewing.io / Pramp / Exponent
- Pramp: free peer mocks, on Exponent platform since July 2024; no AI feedback. Exponent: ~$99/mo or $299/yr (roundup), PM/tech focus, lessons + peer/expert mocks. Interviewing.io: expert human mocks ~$179-$225+/session, coaching $1.5k-$4k; free peer mocks + AI interviewer + recorded replays.
- Sources: https://work-club.com/best-interview-prep-platforms-2026/ , https://www.fast.io/resources/best-ai-interview-prep-tools-2026.md

### LinkedIn (Premium) / Microsoft Copilot
- LinkedIn: role-specific questions generated from real job descriptions; feedback with interview readiness score, strengths/improvements summary, transcript; Premium only, for matched jobs; powered by Azure OpenAI.
- Copilot: generic chat workflow (paste JD, role-play); no dedicated product.
- Sources: https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a8336402 , https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-copilot/for-individuals/do-more-with-ai/ai-for-professional-life/ace-your-job-interview-with-ai-interview-prep

### Teal / Careerflow
- Teal "AI Interview Coach": voice practice, follow-ups, transcription, questions from saved job posting + resume (differ each time), video recording.
- Careerflow: role-specific Qs or upload JD + resume; feedback on content, clarity, confidence; score tracking over time; sessions capped at 5 questions.
- Sources: https://help.careerflow.ai/en/articles/12631590-using-the-ai-mock-interview-tool , https://refreshmiami.com/?p=220173 , https://www.finalroundai.com/blog/teal-ai-interview-practice-review-pros-cons

### 2025-2026 newcomers (small, thin evidence)
- Mocki (panel-style; Coordinator/Interviewer/Evaluator agent split; hackathon origin), InterviewAI (voice, Mar 2026), Apex Interviewer 2026 (SWE coding/system design), Mocksy, BoltPrep (voice + realtime follow-ups).
- Sources: https://hunted.space/product/mocki-2 , https://www.producthunt.com/products/apex-interviewer-2026 , https://www.trendhunter.com/trends/boltprep
- Trend: realtime voice + planner/interviewer/evaluator multi-role architecture.

Gap observed: few competitors clearly combine (a) JD+resume grounding, (b) a transparent multi-dimension rubric, (c) per-answer model answers rewritten from the user's real experience, and (d) a "what was wrong" explanation with evidence quotes. That is our opening.

## 3. Common user complaints (themes)
- Generic/vague feedback ("pleasant but not actionable"); ambiguous scoring.
- Billing: no trial, rebilling, refund refusals, steep monthly price for short-need users.
- Stiff AI personas, thin scenario library; session caps (5 Qs).
- Bugs, unresponsive support, no offline mode.
- Cannot judge technical correctness.
- Trust/ethics concerns around stealth copilots.

## 4. Recommended scoring rubric
Score each answer 1-5 per dimension with anchored descriptors; overall = weighted mean, shown 0-100.

- Behavioral (weights): Relevance to question 20%; STAR structure (Situation, Task, Action, Result) 25%; Specificity/evidence and quantified results 20%; Ownership ("I" vs "we") and impact 15%; Communication clarity/concision 15%; Role/JD alignment 5%.
- Technical/role knowledge: Accuracy 35%; Depth and trade-offs 20%; Reasoning structure 15%; Practical examples 15%; Communication 15%.
- Situational/motivational: relevance, reasoning, company/role fit, communication.
- Voice phase adds delivery: pace (wpm), filler rate, pauses (objective counts, not LLM guesses).

Consistency and fairness:
1. Anchored rubric with 1/3/5 descriptors and a required evidence quote per dimension; score only from what is in the transcript.
2. Structured output (JSON schema): per-dimension score, evidence quote, issue, fix; validate against schema; temperature ~0-0.2.
3. Two-pass: extract facts first (is each STAR element present Y/N), then score from the extraction; deterministic mapping from checklist to score where possible.
4. Calibration set: 30-50 human-graded sample answers across quality levels; run in CI on every prompt/model change; track agreement (e.g. within +-1 on 80%+) and drift. Consider the median of 3 samples for the final score.
5. Fairness: grade content, not accent/grammar/fluency, unless the dimension is delivery; strip name/demographics from grader input; do not penalize non-native phrasing; offer a "delivery excluded" toggle; avoid scoring video appearance, eye contact or body language (bias and validity risk).
6. Honesty guardrails: model answers must use only facts from the user's resume or stated experience; if there is no real experience, give a template with placeholders, never fabricate achievements. Label scores as practice feedback, not hiring predictions.
7. Show progress relative to the user's own prior attempts.

## 5. Recommended session flow
1. Setup: paste JD (or URL), pick/confirm resume, interview type (behavioral / technical / mixed / recruiter screen), seniority, number of questions (5/8/12), mode (text now, voice later), per-question time target. Parse JD into competencies + must-have skills; map resume strengths and gaps.
2. Plan: generate a question blueprint (competency coverage matrix; mix of resume-probing, JD-specific, behavioral, situational, one gap/weakness question). Tell the user what will be covered.
3. Question loop: one question at a time; soft timer (e.g. 2-3 min target, not a hard cutoff); allow skip, repeat, and "hint" (flagged in score).
4. Adaptive follow-ups: after each answer a lightweight check finds missing STAR elements, vague claims or unquantified results; ask max 1-2 follow-ups ("What was your specific role?", "What was the measurable outcome?"). Cap follow-ups per session for pacing.
5. Wrap: optional "do you have questions for us?" round.
6. Report: overall score + per-dimension chart; per-question card = your answer, scores with evidence quotes, what was wrong, model answer tailored to your resume, one-line fix; top 3 priorities; competency coverage vs JD; retry suggestions; export PDF; history.
7. Retry loop: re-answer a question and show score delta.

Recommendation: Phase 2a = MediaRecorder + batch STT + TTS (cheap, keeps the text pipeline and rubric unchanged). Phase 2b = streaming STT for live feel. Realtime speech-to-speech only if conversion data justifies cost; score on the transcript either way. Get mic consent, state retention policy, do not store audio by default. Skip video analysis (bias/validity).

## 7. Feature suggestions
1. JD + resume grounded question blueprint with visible competency coverage map: core differentiator; counters "generic" complaints.
2. Anchored multi-dimension rubric with evidence quotes per score: transparency; fixes "ambiguous feedback".
3. Personalized model answers built from the user's own resume facts (placeholders where no facts): unique vs Google/Yoodli; ties to our resume product.
4. "What went wrong" diagnostic labels (missing Result, no metrics, "we" vs "I", off-topic): actionable.
5. Adaptive 1-2 follow-ups probing weak STAR elements: realism like Sidekick/Teal.
6. Retry-this-answer with score delta and attempt history/progress dashboard (Careerflow tracks scores): retention.
7. Resume feedback loop: gaps found in interview -> suggested resume bullet rewrites / stories to add: leverages our core product.
8. Interview types and personas (recruiter screen, hiring manager, panel, tough/friendly) with difficulty setting: addresses stiff personas.
9. Soft timers and answer-length guidance (90-120s target) with pacing feedback; no hard cutoffs.
10. Practice-weakest-competencies mode and saved question bank with spaced repetition.
11. Transparent pricing: free first session (e.g. 3 questions with full report), low cost credits or monthly, easy cancel/refund: exploits competitor billing complaints.
12. Integrity stance: explicit "practice-only" policy, no stealth/overlay features, privacy controls (delete transcripts, no audio retention by default), score disclaimers.

## 8. Ethics position
Live copilots (Final Round live mode, Interview Sidekick Stealth Mode, Verve, LockedIn) hide AI answers from screen share during real interviews; this risks rescinded offers, violates many employer rules and erodes hiring trust. We should be practice-only: no real-time interview overlay, no screen-share evasion, no capture of real interviews, and say so in marketing/ToS.
