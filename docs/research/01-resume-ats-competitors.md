# 01 - Resume / ATS Optimizer Competitor Research
Date: 2026-10-20. Method: web search + fetch of official pages where reachable.

**Confidence caveat:** Most pricing/complaint info comes from third-party and often competitor-written review blogs (affiliate bias). Official pages fetched successfully: Rezi pricing, Simplify+ help article, LinkedIn help article. Jobscan, Teal, Resume Worded, Enhancv, Kickresume, Careerflow official pricing pages were NOT retrievable (redirect/429/404/cert), so their prices are "reported" and must be re-verified before we benchmark. Reddit/Trustpilot/G2 were not directly readable; complaints below are secondhand summaries.

## 1. Per-competitor profiles

### Jobscan (market incumbent for ATS scan)
- Core: upload resume + paste JD -> Match Score/report grouped into searchability, hard skills, soft skills, recruiter tips, formatting. Can detect the target company's ATS from a job URL and give ATS-specific tips. Power Edit (in-browser editor with AI phrase suggestions, paid), LinkedIn profile optimization, Kanban job tracker, Chrome extension showing match rate on tracked jobs.
- Score: keyword/phrase overlap with fairly strict matching (reportedly "project management" vs "managed projects" may not match) plus format/parse checks. Not a hiring threshold; reviewers say it encourages "score chasing". Common target advice 75-80%.
- Pricing (reported, unverified): free ~5 scans/month; paid ~$49.95/mo or ~$29.98/mo quarterly-equivalent; higher tier reported ~$89.95. No published refund policy per Rezi's comparison page (competitor claim).
- Complaints: price; score is keyword matching only; misses skills already present; low scores perceived as an upsell tactic.
- Privacy: claims data not shared with third parties without consent; a Chrome Web Store reviewer questioned extension permissions.
- Sources: https://www.jobscan.co/video-jobscan-premium , https://careers.wgu.edu/resources/jobscan/ , https://jobshinobi.com/blog/jobscan-resume-scanner-limitations , https://blog.theinterviewguys.com/?p=10428

### Teal (resume builder + job tracker)
- Core: free resume builder, job tracker, Chrome extension for bookmarking jobs; "Matching Mode" surfaces JD keywords vs resume for a bookmarked job; AI resume generation, cover letters.
- Score: keyword comparison vs saved JD; formula not public. Free shows only top 5 keywords per job; Teal+ shows the full list.
- Pricing (reported): Teal+ ~$29/mo, ~$79/quarter, ~$15/mo annual, weekly ~$13 (sources conflict). Free: unlimited resumes/tracking, 10 templates, limited AI credits.
- Positioning: all-in-one job search workspace; does not auto-apply.
- Sources: https://tealhq.helpscoutdocs.com/article/21-using-matching-mode , https://blog.loopcv.pro/teal-hq-review/ , https://gptprompts.ai/teal-ai-resume-builder-review

### Rezi
- Core: ATS-focused AI resume builder/editor, Rezi Score, keyword targeting, content analysis, AI writer, cover letters, AI interview practice, monthly expert review (Pro).
- Score: proprietary "Rezi Score" rubric (criteria not retrieved). One review calls it "oversold".
- Pricing (OFFICIAL, fetched): Free $0 (1 resume, 1 AI interview, 3 PDF downloads, limited score/keywords); Pro $29/mo; Lifetime $149 one-time; Enterprise $99/mo per 200 users. 30-day money-back guarantee.
- Differentiators: lifetime plan, plain ATS-safe templates, team/edu plan.
- Sources: https://www.rezi.ai/pricing , https://scoutify.com/blog/rezi-review/

### Resume Worded
- Core: Score My Resume (rubric on impact, brevity, style), LinkedIn profile review, targeted JD matching, bullet rewriting.
- Pricing (unverified): Pro reported ~$49/mo, ~$33/mo quarterly, ~$19/mo annual; price shown only after account creation.
- Complaints: billing/cancellation and refunds (Paddle-processed); some reviewers say general LLMs give comparable feedback for free. Trustpilot rating high, but a competitor claims review solicitation was flagged (unverified).
- Sources: https://www.trustpilot.com/review/resumeworded.com?page=3 , https://www.loopcv.pro/directory/resume-worded/

### Enhancv
- Core: design-forward builder, content "health check" on bullets (impact vs duty), ATS checker with compatibility score, JD gap analysis.
- Pricing (conflicting): ~$19.99-29/mo monthly, ~$13-16.5/mo semi-annual; 7-day trial, reportedly no permanent free plan (free = 1 resume, watermark, no DOCX per VisualCV, a competitor). Specific fixes paywalled.
- Complaints: generic-sounding AI suggestions; pricey relative to AI parity; designed templates raise ATS-friendliness questions.
- Sources: https://pricingsaas.com/companies/enhancv , https://www.visualcv.com/blog/visualcv-vs-enhancv/

### Kickresume
- Core: templates, AI writer, ATS Resume Checker and tailoring (mid tier), cover letters, portfolio sites.
- Pricing (reported, EUR): ~EUR 24/mo monthly down to ~EUR 10/mo yearly; free is limited (1 template, no AI credits per StylingCV). 14-day money-back reported.
- Complaints: limited free tier, AI hallucinations, ATS parsing issues with complex templates (single review site).
- Sources: https://www.capterra.com/p/180489/Kickresume/ , https://stylingcv.com/blog/kickresume-review-2026-features-pricing-pros-cons-worth-it/

### Careerflow ("Kadoa" not found as a resume product; assumed Careerflow)
- Core: AI resume builder/tailoring with scoring and keyword analysis, LinkedIn optimizer, job tracker, autofill extension, cover letters, LinkedIn-to-resume converter.
- Pricing (conflicting): Capterra from $14.41/mo; Jobright's review says $23.99/mo. Free: 1 tailored resume, basic keyword match, track 10 jobs.
- Complaints: bugs and time-consuming setup (mixed). Capterra 4.8/5 from ~20 reviews.
- Sources: https://www.capterra.com/p/10015230/Careerflow-ai/ , https://jobright.ai/blog/careerflow-review-2026-features-pricing-and-user-experience/

### Simplify (Copilot / Simplify+)
- Core: free autofill extension. Simplify+ adds tailored resumes per job, bullet rewrite, missing-keyword flags, match-to-JD indicator, post-tailoring AI feedback, cover letters, application-question answers, Autopilot (20 auto-applies/week), AI email, referral paths.
- Pricing (OFFICIAL help page): $19.99/1 week, $39.99/1 month, $89.99/3 months; same features on all; auto-renews. Copilot output can be reviewed/edited; Autopilot submits without review.
- Source: https://help.simplify.jobs/articles/5623502-whats-included-in-simplify-features-and-pricing

### Zety / Novoresume (brief)
- Zety: template builder with low-cost trial (~$1.95-2.95, 14 days) converting to recurring ~$25.95 per 4 weeks; auto-renewal is the dominant complaint; free tier very limited. Novoresume: reported ~$23.99/mo, $49.99/qtr, $199.99/yr, marketed as no auto-renew. Both builder-first, weak on JD-specific optimization.
- Sources: https://www.loopcv.pro/directory/zety/ , https://novoresume.com/career-blog/novoresume-vs-zety ; similar trial-trap complaints on Resume.io: https://www.trustpilot.com/review/resume.io?page=6

### LinkedIn AI resume features
- "Tailor my resume to this job": Premium-only, desktop-only, English-only; upload resume, get JD-specific suggestions, edit, then export or attach to application. Warns output "may contain inaccuracies". Help page is silent on storage/training use of uploaded resumes. Conversational AI job search for Premium reported by press (undated).
- Sources: https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a6813101 , https://mezha.media/en/?p=218575

### Newcomers / adjacent (2025-26)
- Jobright (AI copilot: tailor, cover letter, auto-apply; reported $30-40/mo), Huntr (tracker-first with tailor + AI review; ~$40/mo reported), plus small launches (ResumeAdapter, Resume Tailor, ResuCraft, Jobsresume, Resumy AI) mostly "paste JD -> tailored ATS resume". Trends: bundling into job-search suites and auto-apply; many tailors are thin ChatGPT wrappers. None verified to advertise explicit anti-fabrication guardrails.
- Sources: https://resources.rework.com/tools/ai-tools/best-ai-tools-for-job-seekers-2026 , https://api.huntr.co/blog/best-resume-tailor , https://www.hunted.space/product/resume-tailor-8

## 3. Table-stakes (must have at launch)
1. Resume upload (PDF + DOCX) with reliable parsing and a parsed-field preview/correction step.
2. JD paste (ideally URL import) with extracted must-have vs nice-to-have requirements.
3. Match score with category breakdown (hard skills, soft skills, title, education, formatting/parse, contact info).
4. Missing/present keyword list with where-to-add guidance.
5. ATS-safe formatting checks (columns, tables, headers/footers, fonts, images, dates).
6. AI bullet/summary rewrite in an editor with accept/reject per suggestion.
7. Multiple tailored versions per job; PDF and DOCX export (free tier needs at least basic export).
8. Cover letter generation.
9. Clear free tier and a visible privacy / data-deletion statement.

## 4. Gaps / underserved pain points
- **Score distrust:** inconsistent scores across tools, suspicion of upsell manipulation, recruiters say ATS rarely auto-rejects on a score. No one explains scoring transparently or separates "ATS parse risk" from "human relevance". (https://www.visualcv.com/blog/why-job-seekers-say-ats-scores-feel-misleading-or-ats-score-distrust-on-reddit/ , https://careers.uw.edu/blog/2025/07/30/why-you-didnt-hear-back-how-applicant-tracking-systems-score-rank-and-quietly-reject-job-seekers/)
- **Fabrication / keyword stuffing:** tools nudge users to add skills they lack; none visibly enforces evidence-grounding. Our "no fabrication" mandate is under-served.
- **Lexical keyword matching:** misses synonyms; few evaluate seniority, responsibilities and experience gaps.
- **Generic AI voice:** distinctiveness is handled mostly by visual templates, not content.
- **Billing dark patterns:** trial-to-subscription traps (Zety, Resume.io), hidden pricing (Resume Worded), thin refunds.
- **Paywalled fixes:** score is free, actionable fixes are paid.
- **Privacy opacity:** resumes are PII-heavy; LinkedIn's page is silent on storage; competitors give one-line claims.
- **Fragmented workflow:** resume tool vs tracker vs tailoring vs LinkedIn are separate products.
- **Honest gap guidance:** little help when the user genuinely lacks a required skill (reframe adjacent experience, upskill, or skip).

## 5. Feature suggestions for our app
1. **Evidence-linked suggestions:** every AI rewrite/keyword cites the resume line or user-confirmed fact it derives from; anything else is flagged "needs your confirmation" (answers fabrication fear).
2. **Two-axis score:** separate "ATS parse/format health" (deterministic, explainable) from "role relevance" (semantic); show sub-scores and method to beat black-box distrust.
3. **Semantic keyword matching with synonym credit:** "managed projects" counts for "project management", with the reason shown; fixes the lexical-match complaint.
4. **Requirement coverage map:** JD requirements marked Covered / Weak / Adjacent / Genuine gap, with honest next steps instead of stuffing.
5. **Fact bank ("career vault"):** user-confirmed achievements and metrics reused across per-job versions; AI draws only from it, so tailoring is fast without invention.
6. **Recruiter 6-second view:** simulated skim showing the top signals a recruiter sees, plus impact/metric checks per bullet (recruiter readability).
7. **Distinctiveness/voice pass:** flag cliches and generic phrasing, propose specific quantified alternatives in the user's own tone; ATS-safe layout variants.
8. **Parse transparency view:** show what an ATS-style parser extracted (sections, dates, text) next to the original so users can trust and fix parsing.
9. **Diff-based editing with version history:** accept/reject each change, per-job versions, one-click revert.
10. **Privacy-first posture:** no training on user data, auto-delete option, PII redaction before LLM calls, plain-language data page, one-click export/delete.
11. **Fair pricing:** free tier with real fixes, one-click cancel, no trial trap, public price page, optional short pass or lifetime option.
12. **Grounded cover letter and application answers** built from the fact bank with the same evidence links, plus a lightweight tracker (stage + linked resume version) rather than a full CRM.

## 6. Pricing observations
- Dominant model: freemium plus $19-50/mo subscription; heavy discounts for quarterly/annual (Resume Worded ~$49 to ~$19; Teal ~$29 to ~$15; Kickresume EUR24 to EUR10).
- Short passes fit the 1-3 month job-search cycle: Simplify+ weekly $19.99 (official), Teal weekly (reported); churn once hired.
- Rezi is the lone prominent lifetime offer ($149 official) plus a team/edu plan ($99/mo per 200 users): a B2B2C channel (universities, bootcamps, outplacement).
- Paywall pattern: free gives score + limited keywords + few exports; fixes, unlimited scans and DOCX export are paid, which breeds resentment.
- Trial-to-high-recurring-price is the main source of billing complaints (Zety, Resume.io); Novoresume markets no auto-renew.
- Anchors: Jobscan highest (~$50/mo reported), mid cluster $20-30/mo, builders $10-15/mo, auto-apply suites $30-40/mo. A $15-25/mo or short-pass model with a genuinely useful free tier would be competitive.
- Only Rezi and Simplify prices are official; re-check others live.
